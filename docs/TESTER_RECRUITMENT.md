# Finding the first staging testers

Ask for one small action: a twenty-minute observed onboarding session on a
staging shop. Target WooCommerce developers and agencies who already maintain
staging copies. An open-ended request for contacts makes the reader do more
work. Lead with what is being tested; explain x402/MPP after someone is interested.

Useful places, checked September 21, 2026:

- [Woo's agency directory](https://woocommerce.com/development-services/): choose
  five agencies whose work fits your product; personalize a short introduction
  and ask whether one developer can try onboarding on a synthetic staging copy.
- [WordPress Meetup Berlin](https://wpmeetups.de/meetup/berlin/): ask the organizer
  about a short demo or an introduction to a WooCommerce developer.
- [Post Status](https://poststatus.com/about/): a members-only community of
  WordPress professionals. Consider it if you will also participate and learn;
  membership is not a guarantee of testers.
- [Woo community events and office hours](https://woocommerce.com/community/):
  raise a concrete integration or onboarding question in the appropriate event.
- Your hackathon organizers and judges: ask for one warm introduction to a
  developer maintaining a WooCommerce shop.

Follow each community's promotion rules. Do not repost broadly or contact
people on someone else's behalf without permission.

## Suggested post

Hi! I'm looking for one or two WooCommerce developers or agencies with a
staging shop for a 20-minute onboarding test.

I built AgentCart, an open-source WooCommerce plugin that lets a buyer's AI
agent discover products, compare quotes and place an order after the buyer's
approval. It won a hackathon, and I've tested it on my own staging shops. Now
I want to find out where installation and setup are confusing on someone
else's setup.

This session would use synthetic customer data and test payments only—no live
shop or real money. I'll join the session, help with setup and work through any
issues we find. I'd especially like feedback on the setup instructions and
compatibility with your existing plugins.

Would you be willing to try it, or introduce me to one developer who might?
Repository: https://github.com/GiraeffleAeffle/agentcart-tempo-shopbridge

Thanks! Max
