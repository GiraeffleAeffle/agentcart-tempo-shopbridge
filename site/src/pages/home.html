Agent commerce for WooCommerce

A commerce bridge between shops and buyer agents.

AgentCart ShopBridge lets opt-in WooCommerce merchants expose products, calculate final quotes, and accept approval-bound orders from buyer agents. WooCommerce remains the shop’s system of record.

Three independent parts

The WooCommerce plugin

The merchant chooses which products to expose. ShopBridge provides machine-readable catalog, quote, order and status endpoints while WooCommerce handles stock, tax, shipping and fulfillment.

The buyer agent skill

ShopBridge Direct discovers verified merchants and compares their quotes without an AgentCart service. The optional AgentCart service adds durable household policy, approval state and audit records.

Trust and payment

The on-chain merchant registry on Tempo Moderato testnet binds merchant identity and discovery records. An external verifier confirms quote-bound payments before WooCommerce marks an order paid.

From discovery to aftercare

  1. Discover verified merchants from the on-chain registry.
  2. Compare quotes using only country and postcode. Refresh the selected merchant’s quote with the complete delivery address.
  3. A human approves the exact approval packet: merchant, items, total, delivery, payment destination and approval hash.
  4. Hand the approved payment request to the buyer’s wallet or payment provider.
  5. The merchant’s external verifier confirms settlement against the quote.
  6. WooCommerce marks the order paid only after successful verification.
  7. Read order status and tracking, and prepare aftercare requests for the merchant.
